About Me

Teaching, designing, and leading from Mansoura.

I'm a second-year Biology STEM education undergraduate at Mansoura University, where I serve as General Batch Leader and Biology Specialization Leader. I teach Arabic to learners around the world on Preply, and I design presentations that turn complex ideas into clear, memorable stories on Fiverr and Khamsat.
